Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back": one line,
1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the
wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

## The entry

```
You howl, you wish, you promise you'll change tomorrow. Four billion years of it — and not one of you has ever wished ME a good night.
```

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (134 characters, one line).

## Why this line

It answers all three things the prompt names — the howls, the wishes, and
the late-night promise to change tomorrow — in the Moon's own voice: wry,
four billion years old, and a little tired of being wished upon. The
comeback turns at the end: after all that wishing, nobody has ever wished
the Moon itself a good night, a pun on the one thing the Moon owns.
